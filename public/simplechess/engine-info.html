Stockfish 18 Lite

The evaluation is always from White’s perspective: positive favors White, negative favors Black. A score of +1.00 is roughly a one-pawn advantage. M3 means a calculated mate in three moves; the sign indicates which side has the advantage.

This is the lightweight edition of Stockfish 18, running locally in your browser. During playback, the engine briefly evaluates each position. While paused, it continues analyzing and shows the search depth alongside the score. Changing moves stops the previous analysis; leaving the tab suspends it. The evaluation is an estimate and may change with deeper analysis. Your game is never uploaded to a server.
